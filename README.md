# Atieh Abedin, Ph.D.

Computational biologist at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center. I build single-cell and spatial atlases to understand how tissues form, and I use the same approaches to study disease.

## What I'm working on

- **Developmental atlas of the mouse bladder and genitourinary tract.** I'm the sole analyst on this project. It combines single-nucleus multiome (RNA + ATAC) with Visium HD spatial transcriptomics to map how cell types emerge, where they sit in the tissue, and which transcription factors drive them.
- **Tissue context and the human immunopeptidome.** I'm studying how tissue of origin shapes the peptides presented by HLA molecules, with implications for cancer immunology. The manuscript is close to submission.
- **Pan-cancer survival analysis.** I'm collaborating on a survival study across multiple cancer types.

Code for unpublished projects is private until publication. If you're reviewing my work, I'm happy to share access. Just reach out.

## How I work

- Single-cell and single-nucleus multiomics: integration, clustering, gene regulatory networks
- Spatial transcriptomics (Visium HD)
- Trajectory and lineage analysis (Monocle 3)
- Cell–cell communication (CellChat)
- Reproducible pipelines and clear, publication-ready figures

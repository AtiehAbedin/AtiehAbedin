# Atieh Abedin-Do, Ph.D.

Computational biologist working on single-cell and spatial multiomics. I came to computation from the bench, so I've designed assays, built tissues and run clinical genetics panels. Today I build atlases that show how tissues form and what goes wrong in disease, from raw data to publication-ready figures.

Postdoctoral Scientist at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center · Los Angeles, CA

## Current work

**Developmental atlas of the mouse bladder and genitourinary tract** *(first author, manuscript in preparation)*
I'm the lead analyst on a single-nucleus multiome (RNA + ATAC) and Visium HD atlas covering 114,000+ nuclei and 838,000+ spatially resolved cells across five developmental timepoints. My role covers study design, integration, gene regulatory network inference, trajectory analysis and spatially gated cell–cell communication. The aim is to find the transcription factors and signaling pathways that drive each lineage, then prioritize the strongest candidates for validation at the bench.

**Tissue context and the human immunopeptidome** *(first author, close to submission)*
This project asks how tissue of origin shapes the peptides that HLA molecules present to the immune system, which bears on cancer immunology.

> Code for both projects is private until publication. If you're reviewing my work, I'm happy to give you access. Just get in touch.
